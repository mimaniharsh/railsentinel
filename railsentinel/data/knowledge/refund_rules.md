---
title: Cancellation and refund rules for reserved tickets
source: Indian Railways, "Revised Refund Rules w.e.f. 12.11.2015"

## Confirmed tickets cancelled more than 48 hours before departure
A confirmed reserved ticket cancelled more than 48 hours before the scheduled departure of the train attracts a flat cancellation charge per passenger. The charge is Rs 240 for AC First Class and Executive Class, Rs 200 for AC 2-tier and First Class, Rs 180 for AC 3-tier, AC Chair Car and AC 3 Economy, Rs 120 for Sleeper Class, and Rs 60 for Second Class.

## Confirmed tickets cancelled between 48 hours and 12 hours before departure
If a confirmed ticket is cancelled within 48 hours and up to 12 hours before the scheduled departure, the cancellation charge is 25% of the fare, subject to the minimum flat charge for that class.

## Confirmed tickets cancelled between 12 hours and 4 hours before departure
If a confirmed ticket is cancelled less than 12 hours and up to 4 hours before the scheduled departure, the cancellation charge is 50% of the fare, subject to the minimum flat charge for that class.

## Confirmed tickets within 4 hours of departure
No refund is given on a confirmed ticket if it is not cancelled at least 4 hours before the scheduled departure of the train.

## RAC and waitlisted tickets
An RAC or waitlisted ticket can be cancelled for a refund up to 30 minutes before the scheduled departure of the train. A clerkage charge of Rs 60 per passenger is deducted, or Rs 30 per passenger for unreserved second class. No refund is given after that deadline.

## Partially confirmed tickets
The cancellation deadline for partially confirmed tickets is 30 minutes before the scheduled departure of the train.

## Cancelled trains and e-tickets
Since 1 July 2015, when a train is cancelled, the refund for an e-ticket is processed automatically and the passenger does not need to file a TDR. Counter tickets follow the existing procedure.

## Keep in mind
These rules were revised with effect from 12 November 2015 according to the Indian Railways page cited here. Refund rules can be updated, so passengers should confirm the current rules on IRCTC before cancelling.

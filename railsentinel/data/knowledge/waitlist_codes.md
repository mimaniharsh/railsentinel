## GNWL - General Waiting List
GNWL applies when a ticket is booked from the originating station or another major station in the General quota. It is the largest quota and usually clears the fastest of the three common waiting list codes, so its confirmation chances are the highest.

## RLWL - Remote Location Waiting List
RLWL applies to important intermediate stations that have their own dedicated quota. It usually clears faster than PQWL but slower than GNWL, so its confirmation chances are medium.

## PQWL - Pooled Quota Waiting List
PQWL applies to smaller intermediate stations that share one small pooled quota. It is usually the slowest of the three to clear, so its confirmation chances are the lowest.

## TQWL - Tatkal Quota Waiting List
TQWL is the waiting list for tickets booked under the Tatkal quota.

## Keep in mind
How fast a waiting list clears depends on cancellations on that train and date. RailSentinel's confirmation predictor estimates the chance for a specific RailSentinel PNR, but the final status is decided at chart preparation.

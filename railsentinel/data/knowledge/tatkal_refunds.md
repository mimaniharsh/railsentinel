## Confirmed Tatkal tickets
A confirmed Tatkal ticket is generally not refunded when the passenger cancels it. Indian Railways allows a few exceptions, listed below.

## Waitlisted Tatkal tickets
For waitlisted Tatkal tickets, cancellation charges are deducted as per the existing railway rules. Partial cancellation of Tatkal e-tickets is allowed.

## Full refund of fare and Tatkal charges
A full refund, including the Tatkal charges, is possible when the train is delayed by more than 3 hours at the passenger's journey originating station, when the train runs on a diverted route and the passenger does not want to travel, when the train runs on a diverted route that does not serve the boarding or destination station, when the Tatkal coach is not attached and no accommodation is provided in the same class, or when the passenger is given a lower class and does not want to travel.

## Travelling in a lower class
If the passenger travels in a lower class than booked, the difference in fare and in Tatkal charges, if any, is refunded.

## Keep in mind
This summary comes from a travel publisher, not an official Indian Railways page. Passengers should confirm the current Tatkal refund rules on IRCTC.

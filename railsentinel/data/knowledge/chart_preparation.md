## What the reservation chart is
The reservation chart is prepared before a train departs. At chart preparation, waitlisted and RAC passengers learn whether their ticket has been confirmed, moved to RAC or remains waitlisted.

## Chart timing for journeys from 30 December 2025
According to reporting on a Railway Board circular, for journeys starting on or after 30 December 2025 the first reservation chart for trains departing between 05:01 and 14:00 is prepared by 20:00 on the previous day. For trains departing between 14:01 and 23:59, and between 00:00 and 05:00, the first chart is prepared at least 10 hours before departure.

## Earlier changes
Before this change, the first chart had been moved to 8 hours before departure in mid-2025, as reported by Onmanorama on 29 June 2025. Before that, the first chart was prepared about 4 hours before departure.

## Why it matters for waitlisted passengers
An earlier chart means waitlisted passengers know sooner whether they have a confirmed berth and can make other plans if they do not.

## Keep in mind
Chart timings are set by Indian Railways and can change. Passengers should check the current chart status for their train on IRCTC or the official enquiry channels.
